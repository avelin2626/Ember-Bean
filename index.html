Our Roasting Philosophy

Rooted in Good Coffee.

Founded in Jaipur by a collective of architects and Q-graders, Ember & Bean was born out of a shared obsession: marrying the precision of specialty roasting with the timeless warmth of Indian hospitality.

Every bean is hand-selected from high-altitude estates across Chikmagalur and the Nilgiris, roasted with meticulous thermal profiles, and brewed to honor the earth it came from. We believe that true luxury lies in restraint, balance, and mindful craft.
