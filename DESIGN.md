## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the sensory depth and quiet prestige of artisanal coffee culture. It establishes an intimate, tactile, and editorial atmosphere rooted in craftsmanship, architectural warmth, and refined hospitality. 

### Core Aesthetic: Tactile Editorial Luxury
Blending the tactile richness of third-wave specialty roasteries with high-end editorial publication layouts, the system avoids cold minimalism. Instead, it relies on deep roast tones, radiant amber accents, nuanced cream typography, and layered spatial depth. The emotional experience balances quiet contemplation, warmth, and bespoke indulgence.

### Target Audience & Touchpoints
- **Connoisseurs & Epicures:** Discerning patrons seeking artisanal single-origin coffees, curated bean subscriptions, and seasonal pairings.
- **Atmospheric Loungers:** Individuals drawn to elevated lifestyle spaces, design-forward cafés, and slow living.
- **Experience:** Curated tasting notes, bean roast profiles, editorial stories, cellar reserves, and seamless mobile-first hospitality ordering.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around dark roast extraction, radiating embers, and velvet cremas. Built inherently in dark mode, surfaces sink into rich, warm-black tones rather than sterile grays, illuminated by amber and soft vanilla highlights.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#C88A58` / `#DDA15E` / `#B87333`):** Warm roasted caramel and burnished brass. Used for primary calls-to-action, key badges, glowing focus indicators, and focal headlines.
- **Foundation & Surfaces:**
  - **Base Canvas (`#120F0D`):** Deep espresso grounding layer.
  - **Surface Container Base (`#2D1F16`):** Primary card and section container tone, offering a rich, warm roasted brown foundation.
  - **Surface Elevated (`#231C18`):** Elevated cards, menus, popovers, and dialogs.
  - **Surface Interactive (`#2D241E` / `#382E26`):** Active states, input backgrounds, and hover fills.
- **Text & Editorial Accents:**
  - **Display & High Emphasis (`#F7F3EE`):** Soft cream, delivering luxurious, glare-free readability.
  - **Body & Medium Emphasis (`#EDE6DD`):** Warm vanilla for uninterrupted long-form reading.
  - **Muted & Low Emphasis (`#D8CFC4`):** Soft parchment for timestamps, secondary labels, and metadata.
- **Subtle Boundaries:**
  - **Borders & Rules (`#3E332B`, `#4D3F35`):** Warm taupe borders providing structural definition without harsh delineation.

## Typography

The typography balances the editorial authority of high-contrast serif headlines with the pristine legibility of a contemporary geometric sans-serif.

### Pairing Rationale
- **Headings & Display:** Set in **Playfair Display**. It infuses literary gravitas, reminiscent of bespoke coffee anthologies and culinary journals. Italic styles can be selectively deployed for origin notes, tasting notes, and evocative quotes (e.g., *Notes of bergamot & blood orange*).
- **Body & Functional UI:** Set in **Outfit**. Its clean, open apertures ensure frictionless legibility on compact displays, nutritional information, cart summaries, and operational parameters.
- **Labels & Overlines:** Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.06em` to `0.1em`) to create crisp categorizations that mirror luxury packaging design.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model combines an asymmetric magazine grid with deliberate whitespace, avoiding dense enterprise data grids in favor of immersive, spacious product narratives.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid constrained by a `1280px` max-width. Columns utilize `2rem` gutters with `4rem` outer margins. Alternating offsets (e.g., spanning 7 columns for imagery and 5 columns for roasting notes) produce dynamic editorial pacing.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` margins. Complex dual-column narratives collapse gracefully into stacked, unified cards.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins. Content aligns along a continuous vertical thread, with horizontal swipe ribbons for tasting carousels and bean collections.

### Composition Rules
- **Asymmetry:** Primary feature sections should stagger imagery and narrative blocks, offsetting images slightly above or below neighboring text boundaries.
- **Breathability:** Generous vertical padding (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`) frames hero narratives and tasting menus, ensuring a slow, deliberate browsing cadence.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through rich tonal layering, warm ambient glows, and dark espresso shadows rather than stark drop shadows or bright border outlines.

### Depth System
1. **Atmospheric Canvas (Level 0):** Flat background in `#120F0D`. Receives a subtle radial background gradient with warm amber light pooling at `radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% -10%, rgba(200, 138, 88, 0.08), transparent 70%)`.
2. **Resting Cards (Level 1):** Tone `#2D1F16` surfaced with a `1px` border in `#3E332B`. Shadow: `0 10px 30px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.
3. **Interactive Lift & Focus (Level 2):** Tone `#231C18`. Border transitions to `#4D3F35` or `#C88A58/40`. Shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 20px -5px rgba(200, 138, 88, 0.12)`.
4. **Modals, Drawers & Cart Overlays (Level 3):** Deep espresso layered over a frosted backdrop (`backdrop-blur-md bg-[#120F0D]/80`). Shadow: `0 25px 60px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85), inset 0 1px 1px rgba(247, 243, 238, 0.05)`.

## Shapes

The shape system employs soft, welcoming geometric curves that evoke polished river stones, curved ceramic espresso cups, and hand-finished walnut counters.

### Curvature Tokens
- **Standard Structural Elements (Cards, Panels, Sheets):** 1rem (`rounded-lg`) to 1.5rem (`rounded-xl`).
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Standard Action Triggers):** 0.75rem (`rounded-md` to `rounded-lg`).
- **Tasting Chips, Category Tags, & Badges:** Full pill radius (`rounded-full`) for high tactile contrast against rectangular content blocks.
- **Image Frames:** Paired with soft inner radiuses (`1.5rem`) and hairline containment borders (`1px solid #3E332B`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Burnished Amber"):** Gradient background from `#C88A58` to `#B87333` with high-contrast text in `#120F0D`. Finished with `rounded-full` or `rounded-xl`, subtle ember hover glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(200, 138, 88, 0.35)`), and smooth transition.
- **Secondary ("Smoked Espresso"):** Background `#231C18` with a `1px solid #4D3F35` border. Text in `#F7F3EE`. On hover, the border illuminates to `#C88A58` with text shifting to `#DDA15E`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Text in `#EDE6DD` with an animated amber underline expanding from center.

### Chips & Badges
- **Tasting Note Pills:** Background `#231C18`, text `#D8CFC4`, hairline border `#3E332B`. Rounded full (`rounded-full`), padded `px-3.5 py-1`.
- **Roast Profile Indicators:** Minimalist dot indicators with burnished brass accents `#C88A58` accompanied by small tracked uppercase typography (`label-sm`).

### Lists & Menu Items
- **Café Offerings & Bean Rosters:** Separated by low-opacity hairline dividers (`#3E332B/60`).
- **Structure:** Serif item name on the left in `#F7F3EE`, leader line or empty gutter, tasting note italicized underneath in `#D8CFC4`, and amber price badge on the right in `#DDA15E`.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Form Fields:** Grounded surface `#2D1F16` with border `#3E332B` and border-radius `0.75rem`. Text in `#F7F3EE`.
- **Active / Focus:** Border transitions to `#C88A58` with a subtle amber glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(200, 138, 88, 0.15)`). Placeholders styled in `#D8CFC4/40`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** Base container `#231C18` with `#4D3F35` border. Checked state fills with `#C88A58` carrying an espresso mark (`#120F0D`).
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular frame with concentric amber pulse fill on select.

### Cards
- **Product & Origin Cards:** Background `#2D1F16`, `rounded-2xl`, border `#3E332B`. Includes high-aspect-ratio photography with warm low-key grading, editorial serif headline, caramel price accents, and pill tags for process (e.g., *Washed*, *Natural*, *Anaerobic*).

### Specialized Components
- **Roast Scale Gauge:** Horizontal discrete step-bar styled like brass measuring gauges, indicating levels from *Light Honey* to *Dark Espresso Roast*.
- **Flavor Wheel Summary:** Compact multi-axis radar or grouped pill clusters categorized into *Floral*, *Nutty*, *Caramel*, and *Fruity* notes using warm tinted badges.
